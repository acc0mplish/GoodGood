## Style

- Use 합니다/습니다 style for body text, noun phrases for labels and buttons.
- Do not use 면/너 (second person).
- Keep `{placeholder}`, `<tag>` and `Debugger`, `UseFilter`, `null`, `--flag`, file names exactly.
- Keep CPU, GPU, I/O, PID, RAM, VRAM, SMART, NVMe, PCIe, PawnIO and similar identifiers in Latin script.
